# Marine Debris FLS Dataset

Official source: https://github.com/mvaldenegro/marine-debris-fls-datasets

Modality: Forward-Looking Sonar (FLS)

Relevance: high for marine-debris object detection. Candidate classes include debris/objects such as tires, bottles, cans, chains, hooks, and pipes depending on the released annotations.
